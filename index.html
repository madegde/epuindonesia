Measures of policy-related economic uncertainty using news-based approach from major Indonesian newspapers

News Sources

This study constructs the first comprehensive news-based Economic Policy Uncertainty (EPU) index for Indonesia, covering October 1994 to December 2024. Using the Baker, Bloom, and Davis (2016) methodology, we analyze 6,340 articles from five leading Indonesian newspapers to track policy uncertainty over three decades.

Methodology

Data Collection

Sourced 6,430 articles from the Factiva database covering five leading Indonesian newspapers (The Jakarta Post, LKBN ANTARA, Bisnis Indonesia, Tempo, and The Jakarta Globe) from October 1994 to December 2024. Factiva was selected for its comprehensive local Indonesian newspaper database and timely updates.

EPU Keyword Classification

Each article must contain at least one term from all three categories:
• Economic (E): economic, economy
• Policy (P): policy, public, tax, taxation, taxes, spending, expenditure, fiscal, stimulus, budget, deficit, national debt, tariff, subsidies, subsidy, Bank Indonesia, central bank, reserves, interest rates, legislation, legislative, house of representatives, regulation
• Uncertainty (U): uncertain, uncertainty, uncertainties

Index Construction

Following Baker et al. (2016) methodology with 5 steps:
1) Compute times-series variance σ²ᵢ for each paper
2) Standardize Xᵢₜ by dividing through by standard deviation σᵢ
3) Compute mean over newspapers Yᵢₜ for each month
4) Compute M, the mean value of Zₜ in interval T₂
5) Multiply Zₜ by (100/M) to obtain normalized EPU time-series index

Event Analysis

Analyzed EPU index against major economic and political events including Asian Financial Crisis (1997-1998), political scandals (Bulogate/Bruneigate 2000-2001), constitutional amendments (2002), global financial crisis (2008), fuel subsidy cuts (2014), COVID-19 pandemic (2020), and Russia-Ukraine war (2022) to validate the index's responsiveness to significant events.

Key Findings

Peak Uncertainty Periods

1998 Asian Financial Crisis: EPU peaked at 320
2001 Political Scandals: Wahid impeachment (255)
2020 COVID-19 Pandemic: Global health crisis (228)

Global Events Impact

2008 Global Financial Crisis: Moderate increase
2015 China Economic Turmoil: Significant spike
2022 Russia-Ukraine War: Polycrisis effects

Domestic Policy Events

2014 Fuel Subsidy Cuts: Jokowi's early reforms
2002 Constitutional Amendments: Democratic transition
2004 Election Year: First direct presidential election

Research Impact

First comprehensive EPU index for Indonesia providing insights for policymakers, investors, and researchers studying emerging market policy uncertainty dynamics.
